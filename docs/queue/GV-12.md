# `GV-12` — the phone surface never retries, so a transient outage leaves it dead until a human taps Retry

[← Builder Queue index](../BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

🟠 **P1.** Filed 2026-09-08. **Found by the RotaryPhone session while debugging their own outage** —
they were reading our logs alongside theirs and noticed what we did not.

## The evidence, confirmed from both ends

RotaryPhone's GV bridge was dead 14:08–15:31 EDT (~83 minutes). Service was restored at **15:31:17**.

**After restoration, `radio-web` made ZERO further GV calls.** No `GvBridgeApiService` activity in our
logs; no inbound SMS/voicemail requests in theirs. The UI sat on *"Couldn't load…"* against a **fully
healthy backend** until the owner tapped **Retry**, which worked immediately.

**So an 83-minute upstream outage leaves our phone surface permanently dead until a human intervenes,
even after the cause has cleared.**

⭐ It is the mirror image of the honest-status work: **we made failure visible, and then built a
surface that cannot recover from having seen it.**

## What to build

An auto-retry with backoff, or a refetch when the reconnect banner clears. Prefer the latter if the
banner is being touched anyway — one trigger beats two clocks.

## ⛔ The status contract we were given this morning is WRONG — do not use it

RotaryPhone's morning reply (`inbound/2026-09-08-rotaryphone-reply.md:98`) told us to bind to
`degraded` or `authBlackout` and **never** to `available`. **They retracted it the same day**, and the
outage proved it. Live capture during the total outage, while we were getting 502s:

```json
{"available":false,"degraded":false,"authBlackout":false,
 "cookiesValid":false,"lastApiSuccessAt":null}
```

**`degraded` and `authBlackout` both false through a total outage.** A banner bound to either would
have stayed silent for 83 minutes. There are two failure states and that advice covered one: when the
adapter goes **inactive**, the honest fields reset to false because they are **per-activation**.

**Bind to the shape, not to a boolean:**

```
unhealthy = !cookiesValid || !available || degraded || authBlackout
            || lastApiSuccessAt is null or older than ~2 min
```

`cookiesValid:false` held in both states; `lastApiSuccessAt` is the strongest single signal. And
**`authBlackout` can be true for under a second** (920 ms measured, zero true-samples across 411
polls), so **latch it as an event with a minimum display window** — a 10-second poll will never see it.

## ⚠ Read `UI-10` before estimating this

`radio-web`'s own Blazor circuit was timing out every ~30 s throughout, with a
`JSDisconnectedException` at 15:27:06. **A dead circuit cannot refetch even if the code wants to**, so
part of what looks like a missing-retry defect may be a broken transport. Establish which before
building — a retry bolted onto a dying circuit will look like it does not work, and the wrong row will
take the blame.

## Verification

Unit-testable with a fake API service: fail N times, then succeed, and assert the panel refetches
**without user input**. ⚠ **Must fail first** — today it does not refetch at all, so confirm the test
is RED against `main` before trusting it.

⚠ Live confirmation needs the bridge to actually go down and come back. **Do not simulate that on the
box.** The next real outage is the honest test, and this row should say so rather than implying a green
unit test proves recovery.
