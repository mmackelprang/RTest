## The finding (FROM CODE, `main` at `8df918b`)

- `AudioVisualizationHubService` is registered as a **singleton** in `radio-web` (`src/Radio.Web/Program.cs:412`). Every Blazor circuit (every open browser tab on `:5002`, including the kiosk) gets the same instance and the same `HubConnection`.
- Its subscriptions are one plain `HashSet<string>` (`src/Radio.Web/Services/Hub/AudioVisualizationHubService.cs:33`). `SubscribeToSpectrumAsync` adds `"Spectrum"` (`:302`); `UnsubscribeFromSpectrumAsync` removes it (`:316`) **and** invokes `UnsubscribeFromSpectrum` on the one shared connection (`:319`), which takes that connection out of the API's `Spectrum` group (`src/Radio.API/Hubs/AudioVisualizationHub.cs:105-107`). `Levels` (`:326`/`:340`) and `Waveform` (`:350`/`:364`) are the same.
- A set records **whether** something wants a group, not **how many** things do. Two panels subscribing to `Spectrum` leave one entry; the first to unsubscribe removes it and leaves the group for both.
- `VisualizerPanel` unsubscribes its current mode in `DisposeAsync` (`src/Radio.Web/Components/Shared/VisualizerPanel.razor:1102`, via `UnsubscribeFromCurrentMode`, `:540-557`), and on every mode change (`:491`) and the saved-mode move after the preference read (`:411`).

So any one circuit can stop the stream every other circuit is still drawing:

1. **A panel is disposed** — a tab closed, or a reloaded page's old circuit being torn down. A disconnected circuit is kept for up to `DisconnectedCircuitRetentionPeriod` = 10 min (`Program.cs:69`) before disposal, so the unsubscribe can arrive well after the new page has subscribed.
2. **A panel changes mode** — circuit B moving Spectrum → Waveform unsubscribes `Spectrum` while circuit A is still showing Spectrum. *(Read from code; not observed.)*

The surviving panel gets no error: the connection stays up, its handlers stay attached, and frames simply stop. It sits on "Waiting for audio data" (or on its last frame, if it had data) until something re-subscribes it — the panel's own next mode change or a fresh page.

⚠ The reconnect paths do not repair it. `Reconnected` (`:129-160`) and `ReplaySubscriptionsAsync` (`:247-267`) replay the **set**, which the departed panel has already emptied.

## Observed (once, local render, 2026-10-02)

While rendering #765 locally, a page opened about a minute after an earlier test browser closed showed the (new, `UI-30`) disconnected state, cleared it 1.8 s after the hub came back, and then sat on "Waiting for audio data" — consistent with a replay of an empty set. A fresh page received frames at once. Not reproduced deliberately, and **not yet seen on the box**. On the kiosk the likely trigger is a page reload (or a second browser on `:5002` being closed).

## Fix direction (for the plan — NOT decided here)

- **Per-circuit reference counting** in the singleton: count subscribers per group (or track a set of subscriber tokens per group), invoke `SubscribeTo<Group>` on 0 → 1 and `UnsubscribeFrom<Group>` on 1 → 0, and replay every group with a count above zero on reconnect. Smallest change; keeps one connection to the API. A panel that is disposed without having subscribed (or unsubscribes twice) must not drive the count negative — a per-consumer token is safer than a bare integer.
- **or a scoped (per-circuit) hub client**: one `HubConnection` per circuit, so group membership is per circuit on the server. Isolation for free, at the cost of one API connection per open tab — and `UI-19`'s gate counts connections, so that interaction must be checked.
- Either way, `UnsubscribeFromAllAsync` (`:391-399`, clears the whole set) needs the same treatment or removal; nothing in `src/Radio.Web` outside the service calls it today.

## Verification

⛔ **NOT auto-mergeable** — a Web-side lifecycle change on the connection every panel shares.

1. **Unit:** two consumers subscribe `Spectrum`; one unsubscribes (and, separately, one is disposed); assert the service did **not** invoke `UnsubscribeFromSpectrum` and still holds the group for replay. It must fail on `8df918b`. Mutation-check the 1 → 0 boundary.
2. **Mode change:** consumer A on Spectrum, consumer B switches Spectrum → Waveform; A's group survives.
3. **Owner check at the console:** with Spectrum showing on the kiosk, reload the kiosk page (or open a second browser on `http://radio:5002`, show the visualizer, then close it) and wait out the old circuit (up to 10 min); the kiosk's visualizer keeps streaming throughout.
