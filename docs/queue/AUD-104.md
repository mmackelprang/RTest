# `AUD-104` — Fingerprinting on AM and SW: decide whether the radio's SongRec calls run there (owner decision first)

[← Builder Queue index](../BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

Read from code on `main` `2a2893d2`.

## Measured facts

- **The radio always asks for a lookup.** `SoundFlowAudioTap.NeedsFingerprintingLookup`
  (`src/Radio.Infrastructure/Audio/Fingerprinting/SoundFlowAudioTap.cs:107-138`) answers per source type. For
  Radio, Vinyl and USB it returns `true` unconditionally (`:136-137`), whatever the band. The gate is consumed
  at `:178`.
- **The radio is an "unknown-start" source under the #797 call policy.** `FingerprintCallPolicy`
  (`src/Radio.Fingerprinting/Services/FingerprintCallPolicy.cs:99-100`, `IsKnownStart` `:154`) schedules one
  attempt every `UnknownStartIntervalSeconds` (15 s on a 13 s capture since #797), about **240 calls/hour**,
  and that equals the global cap (`MaxCallsPerHour = 240`). An hour on the radio uses the whole budget wherever
  it is tuned.
- **What AM/SW would cost.** Most AM in this area is talk, news and sport, and SW broadcasters are mostly speech.
  SongRec will mostly return no-match, which still counts against the hourly cap and occupies the 13 s capture.
  Music on AM is band-limited (~5 kHz), which lowers the match rate even when it is music. That last point is
  inferred, not measured.

## ⛔ Owner decision (open, not to be invented)

Should the radio fingerprint on AM and SW?

1. **Off on AM and SW** (`NeedsFingerprintingLookup` is false when the radio's band is AM or SW). No calls, no
   album art and no History identifications on those bands. Simplest, and saves the quota.
2. **On, at a slower schedule** for AM/SW (for example every 60 s), so the occasional music station still gets
   identified. Needs a per-band interval in `FingerprintingOptions`.
3. **On, unchanged.** Same as FM: about 240 calls/hour and mostly no-matches.
4. **Configurable per band**, defaulting to one of the above.

The Planner's suggestion, for the owner to accept or reject: **1, behind a config flag**. It costs a missed ID on
the rare AM music station. Option 2 keeps that ID at the cost of a second schedule in the policy. Ask before
building, and record the answer here.

## Scope (once decided)

- Make the gate band-aware for the radio source (it needs the radio's current band, which
  `SDRRadioAudioSource` exposes). A band change counts as a re-tune for the policy (`RequestImmediate`/
  `ResetSegment`), so switching FM → AM stops a pending call and AM → FM starts one promptly.
- Now-playing and History on AM/SW must not keep showing the last FM identification. Check what the panel shows
  when no lookup runs; that is the visible half of this row.
- Tests: the gate per band; a band switch mid-schedule.
