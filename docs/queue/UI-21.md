## What it does (spec §4.8)

While the PRESETS knob's overlay is open (the turn before the press):

- the bar **scrolls the highlighted card into view**;
- that card gets the overlay's highlight treatment — `--surface-hover` fill and a **2 px amber bar along
  the card's bottom edge** (the horizontal form of `.encoder-selector-bar`, `--row-accent` =
  `--source-radio`);
- when the overlay closes, the highlight clears and the bar returns to the active card (subject to
  `UI-20`'s 10 s manual-scroll hold-off).

Tapping a card while the overlay is open recalls that card; the overlay closes on its own idle timer. No
announcement is added — the HUD already has a `role="status"` mirror.

## ⚠ For the Planner to confirm — do not inherit

The spec *believes* the Web already receives everything needed: the full row list and `HighlightIndex`
in the HUD payload (`PresetSelectorService.cs:626-652`; row ids `preset:{id}`, `:336`). It asks the
Planner to confirm that `EncoderHudService` exposes that payload to **a second subscriber** (the bar, in
addition to the HUD). **That is a belief, not a measurement.** Read the delivery path end to end before
planning; if an API or hub change is needed, escalate to the Architect rather than widening this row.

## Verification

- bUnit / service test: a HUD payload with a `HighlightIndex` pointing at a preset row highlights the
  matching card and scrolls it into view; overlay close clears it.
- Owner at the panel: turning the PRESETS knob moves the bar's highlight in step, and the card the knob
  lands on is the one the bar shows.
