# `AUD-108` — startup restore and preset recall still infer the band from the frequency

[← Builder Queue index](../BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

🟡 **P2. Filed 2026-10-12 by the `AUD-105` Builder**, from the second pre-merge review of
[#805](https://github.com/mmackelprang/RTest/pull/805). Read from code; **not measured on the box.** It predates
`AUD-105`, which fixed only the band change.

## The defect (read from code)

`RadioReceiver.TrySetFrequency` always picks the band with `BandPresets.FindBandForFrequency`, the first band in
`AllBands` order, edges included. So any "`SetBandAsync`, then `SetFrequencyAsync`" pair lands on whichever band
owns the frequency first, not the band asked for. The receiver also switches band twice. VHF (30–300 MHz)
contains FM, AIR and WB. AIR's bottom edge, 108 MHz, is FM's top, and VHF's bottom edge, 30 MHz, is SW's top.

`AUD-105` found the same defect on the band change itself (box UAT: VHF → FM at 101.1 MHz). It fixed it with
one `IRadioControl.TuneInBandAsync` in `RadioBandSwitch`. Three callers still use the pair:

| Caller | Where (on `main` after #805) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Startup restore of the saved band and frequency | `SDRRadioAudioSource.cs` ~1112 / ~1120 | A box saved on AIR at 108 MHz, or on VHF at 101.1 MHz, comes back on FM after a restart |
| Preset recall from the encoder | `PresetSelectorService.cs:457-458` | A VHF preset inside FM, AIR or WB recalls onto that band |
| Preset recall from the API | `RadioController.cs:850-851` (`LoadPreset`) | Same |

## Scope

Each caller becomes one `TuneInBandAsync(band, frequency)`, with `RadioBandSwitch`'s fallback to `SetBandAsync`
when the frequency is outside the band. The tests use a real `SDRRadioAudioSource` and `RadioReceiver`
(`RadioControllerBandMemoryTests` shows the shape): a VHF preset at 101.1 MHz stays on VHF, and a restart saved on
AIR at 108 MHz comes back on AIR.

Check the startup path's step restore (`IsAllowedStep`, ~1127) still applies after the change.
