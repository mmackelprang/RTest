## (a) It opens under the area pressed

**Before:** the triple-tap target moved to the slot left of the Home pill in #711, but the tray stayed
pinned to the **top-right corner** (`.dev-tray { top: 0; right: 0 }`) — several hundred pixels from the
finger that opened it.

**Now:**

- `dev-gesture.js` passes the **third tap's** `clientX`/`clientY` and `window.innerWidth`/`innerHeight` to
  `MainLayout.ToggleDevTray`. A touch tap reaches the listener as a synthesized `click`, which carries the
  touch point.
- `DevTrayPlacement.ForTap` (`src/Radio.Web/Components/Shared/DevTrayPlacement.cs`) **centres the
  480 px tray on the tap**, `12 px` below it, **clamped** 12 px inside both side edges; if 440 px (the
  tray's maximum height) does not fit below it **flips above** the tap, expressed as a `bottom` offset so
  the tray's lower edge sits just above the tap whatever height it renders at; if neither side fits it is
  clamped on-screen. A nonsense viewport falls back to 1920×720.
- `DevTray.razor` writes `left`/`top` (or `bottom`), the width and the open height inline. The width and
  height constants live **only** in `DevTrayPlacement`, so the flip decision and the rendered box cannot
  disagree. With no placement the stylesheet's old top-right corner is the fallback.
- Closed, the tray is `visibility: hidden` as well as zero-height, so its border does not paint a line
  wherever it was last anchored.

⚠ **There is still one gesture target** — the invisible 64 × 56 slot left of Home. "Under the area
pressed" is under *where in that slot* you pressed, so two taps move the tray by up to ~64 px, not
across the screen. Measured at 1920×720: taps at x=1018 and x=1063 opened the tray at left 778 and 822.
Moving or multiplying the gesture target is a design change this row did not make.

Toggle semantics are unchanged: a triple-tap while the tray is open closes it; the next one reopens it
under the new tap.

## (b) What is actionable is obvious

Read `design-system.css` §2 tokens and the existing button patterns (`.band-scan-btn`, `.source-bubble`,
`.nav-pill`) first. Two kinds of thing, styled apart:

- **Actions** (`.dev-card.dev-card--action`, all `<button>`s): raised `--surface-elevated` surface with a
  shadow, **accent border** (`--accent-primary` at 45 %, full on hover/press), an **accent icon** at the
  right (flag, download, chevron, toggle), the label in `--text-high`, a one-line hint of what it does in
  `--text-medium` until tapped, a **pressed state** (`--accent-surface` fill, scale 0.98) and a
  focus-visible ring. ≥ 64 px tall (measured 68–107 px), above the 58 px kiosk-dialog standard. The LOG-5
  toggle fills with `--accent-surface` while on.
- **Readings** (Updates/sec, Engine state): moved out of the grid into a flat strip under the header —
  no border, no surface, no icon, and **not buttons** (they were disabled `<button class="dev-card">`s,
  visually identical to the actions).
- The **close ×** is a bordered 48 × 48 target instead of a 20 px glyph — it is the way out.

## Evidence

- `DevTrayPlacementTests` (below, centring, both clamps, flip-above boundary, no-room, narrow and
  nonsense viewports, two taps → two placements); `DevTrayTests` (placement written inline, flip as
  `bottom`, no placement → default; every action is an enabled `<button>` with the action class and icon;
  readings are not buttons and carry no action class); `MainLayoutNavTests` (the gesture callback opens
  the tray under the tap, closes it, and reopens it under a new tap).
- **Mutation-checked:** never flipping, no left clamp, the layout not passing the placement, the tray
  ignoring it, a reading turned into a button, a reading given the card class, an action missing its
  class — **all killed**.
- Local render at 1920×720 (`ui2526-screens/01`–`06`): tray under tap A and under tap B, pressed state.
