## (a) It opens under the area pressed

**Before:** the triple-tap target moved to the slot left of the Home pill in #711, but the tray stayed
pinned to the **top-right corner** (`.dev-tray { top: 0; right: 0 }`) — several hundred pixels from the
finger that opened it.

**Now:**

- `dev-gesture.js` passes the **third tap's** `clientX`, the pressed slot's top and bottom edges, and
  `window.innerWidth`/`innerHeight` to `MainLayout.ToggleDevTray`. A touch tap reaches the listener as a
  synthesized `click`, which carries the touch point.
- `DevTrayPlacement.ForPress` (`src/Radio.Web/Components/Shared/DevTrayPlacement.cs`) **centres the
  480 px tray on the tap's x** and hangs it **8 px below the pressed slot's bottom edge** — so it sits
  under the area pressed without cutting across the HOME/QUEUE pills — **clamped** 12 px inside both
  side edges. If 440 px (the tray's maximum height) does not fit below, it **flips above** the slot,
  expressed as a `bottom` offset so the tray's lower edge sits just above the slot whatever height it
  renders at; if neither side fits it stays on-screen. Off-viewport input is clamped in; a nonsense
  viewport falls back to 1920×720.
- `DevTray.razor` writes `left`/`top` (or `bottom`), the width and the open height inline. The width and
  height constants live **only** in `DevTrayPlacement`, so the flip decision and the rendered box cannot
  disagree. With no placement the stylesheet's old top-right corner is the fallback.
- Closed, the tray is `visibility: hidden` as well as zero-height, so its border does not paint a line
  wherever it was last anchored. Open, a transparent backdrop one tier below it closes it on a tap
  anywhere else.

⚠ **There is still one gesture target** — the invisible 64 × 56 slot left of Home. "Under the area
pressed" is under *where in that slot* you pressed, so two taps move the tray sideways by up to
~64 px, not across the screen. Measured at 1920×720: the slot is at x 1009–1073, y 3–59; taps at
x=1018 and x=1063 opened the tray at left 778 and 822, both at top 67.
Moving or multiplying the gesture target is a design change this row did not make.

Toggle semantics are unchanged: a triple-tap while the tray is open closes it; the next one reopens it
under the new tap.

## (b) What is actionable is obvious

Read `design-system.css` §2 tokens and the existing button patterns (`.band-scan-btn`, `.source-bubble`,
`.nav-pill`) first. Two kinds of thing, styled apart:

- **Actions** (`.dev-card--action`, all `<button>`s): raised `--surface-elevated` surface with a shadow,
  a **full `--accent-primary` border** (the `.band-scan-btn` outlined-action language), an **accent icon**
  at the right (flag, download, chevron, toggle), the label in `--text-high` pinned to the top, a
  one-line hint of what it does in `--text-medium` until tapped, a **pressed state** (`--accent-surface`
  fill, scale 0.98) and a focus-visible ring. Hover styling only on devices that hover, so a tapped card
  does not stay lit. ≥ 64 px tall (measured 68–87 px), above the 58 px kiosk-dialog standard. The LOG-5
  toggle, while on, gets a tinted fill **and** a leading accent bar.
- **Readings** (Updates/sec, Engine state): moved out of the grid into a flat strip under the header —
  no border, no surface, no icon, and **not buttons** (they were disabled `<button class="dev-card">`s,
  visually identical to the actions).
- The **close ×** is a bordered 56 × 56 target instead of a 20 px glyph — it is the way out.
