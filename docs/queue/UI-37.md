## Depends on

**The `feat/house-audio-routes` build of `rnet-pi` on the controller.** Without it `GET /system` has no route, and neither do the source routes or the `X-RNet-Password` header. That is a deploy to a live service, on the owner's timing, and the phone app on port 3000 must be checked after it.

## What it adds

- **House Audio tab** on `/system`, second under System Stats. `IntegrationsTabIndex` and `DiagnosticsTabIndex` moved with it.
- **Room cards:** power, mute, volume, source, Tune. Commands apply at once, through `HouseAudioCommandLedger`.
- **Room settings popover:** Party Mode, Loudness, Do Not Disturb, Bass, Treble, Balance, Turn On Volume, Maximum Volume, Delete Zone.
- **Controller settings:** name, password, sources (name, type, edit, add, delete), About.
- **Sliders act only on a drag.** A tap does nothing (`SeekBar.RelativeDragSlop`, owner ruling). Media scrubbers keep tap-to-seek.

Setup, routes and the kiosk rules: [`docs/integrations.md` § 4](../integrations.md).
