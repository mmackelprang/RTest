## What the owner asked (2026-10-01)

> *"I'd like to consider moving the preset column to a bar across the bottom of the radio with
> touchable arrow pushbuttons to scroll left and right. This would widen the radio control area,
> hopefully providing enough horizontal space for all band buttons on one row, allowing space for a
> preset band above the current two-row band selection area. … It also seems like the scan area could
> be color coded based on signal strength. Consider that as part of a UX enhancement also."*

**"Scan area" = the BAND view's sweep map** (`AUD-76` / `AUD-91`) — the shared reading of the coordinator
and the Designer; the owner did not object (spec, Owner decisions Q6).

## Owner decision: the cool ramp (spec Q5)

Colour is keyed to **dB above the map's median** (the noise estimate `PeakProminenceDb` already uses,
`FmBandMath.cs:32-37`), **not** to the normalised bar height — the same height means 10 dB on a dead map
and 40 dB on a busy one (spec §5.1).

| Tier | dB above median | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Noise | < 6 (= `PeakProminenceDb`) | `--text-low` (grey) |
| Weak | 6 – 12 | `--signal-blue` |
| Fair | 12 – 20 | `--accent-primary` (cyan, today's colour) |
| Strong | ≥ 20 | `--signal-green` |

⚠ **12 and 20 dB are provisional.** Define them as named constants next to `PeakProminenceDb`; the
Builder logs the tier counts from **one real FM map on the box** and the thresholds are fixed only after
that. The RSSI meter's green/amber/red is **not** reused: it would paint the best stations red and hide the
amber "tuned here" station line among the bars (spec §5.2).

## Scope

- **Tiers computed in C#** next to `NormalizeLevels` (unit-testable), passed as one extra field per level
  into `drawBandMap` (`VisualizerPanel.razor`, `visualizer.js`). Web-only; no API change. Tier is per
  channel by level alone, not by the peak rule.
- **Fill under the bars goes neutral grey** — a `--text-medium` gradient, 0.18 → 0.03 alpha — so a cyan
  wash does not tint every bar (§5.3).
- **No extra mark on preset stations.** The dashed preset tick already passes through the bar; recolouring
  it would make the colour lie about strength (§5.3).
- **Small legend as page text** in the status line after the age —
  `FM · scanned 7 h ago · ▮ weak ▮ fair ▮ strong`, 11 px mono, 8 × 8 swatches, noise tier not listed —
  in markup, not on the canvas, so AT-SPI and tests can read it. Hidden when the map is empty or the band
  is out of range (§5.4).
- **Colour-blind safe because height still encodes strength** — colour is a redundant second channel
  (§5.5).
- Draw order unchanged: fill → bars → preset ticks → amber station line and caret.

## Verification

- Unit tests on the tier function at each boundary (just below/at 6, 12, 20 dB above median) and on a
  flat (dead) map.
- bUnit: the legend renders with a map and is absent for an empty map and for AM/SW.
- Box: log the tier counts from a real FM map, then fix or adjust the 12/20 thresholds and record the
  counts in this dossier. Owner look at the panel.
