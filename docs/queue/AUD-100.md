# `AUD-100` — Scan Up/Down and the band map share one station list

[← Builder Queue index](../BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

✅🔬 **SHIPPED 2026-10-04 (Builder, `feat/aud-100-scan-band-map`).** Deployed and SHA-verified on `radio`; the agent's
box UAT passed for the map-driven scan and the fallback. **Owner checks outstanding** (see § Owner UAT). Owner request,
2026-10-04.

> *"There are a couple of ways to scan for stations … Scan Up/Scan Down buttons on the radio control
> console, and … the 'Discover' button in the radio bandwidth visualization panel … This feels a bit like
> duplication."* Owner chose options 1 + 2 of the coordinator's proposal: keep both controls; connect their data.

## The two flows today (read from the controllers only — internals not yet read)

| | Scan Up/Down | Discover |
|---|---|---|
| Endpoint | `POST /api/radio/scan/start`, `/scan/stop` (`RadioController.cs:263`, `:298`) | `POST /api/bandmap/scan` (`BandMapController.cs:69`) |
| Does | Live seek from the current frequency to the next station | Whole-band sweep, output silenced (~21 s FM) |
| Keeps | Nothing | `BandMap` per band: channels, signal, scan time, age (`BandMapService`) |

Neither reads the other's results.

## What to build

1. **Scan reads the map.** When the current band has a map younger than a freshness threshold, Scan
   Up/Down tunes straight to the next mapped channel above/below the current frequency (wrapping at the band
   edge as the live seek does today). No map, a stale map, or no mapped channel in that direction → fall back
   to today's live seek. Pick the threshold and justify it; make it configuration.
2. **Scan writes the map.** Each station a live seek stops on is upserted into that band's map with its
   signal and a timestamp, marked as **seek-observed**, not swept.
   ⚠ **A seek proves presence, never absence.** It skips frequencies without measuring them, so a
   seek-observed entry must not remove or override swept channels, must not reset the map's sweep time or
   age, and must not make a never-swept band look swept. A later full sweep is the authority and replaces
   seek-observed entries.
3. The BAND view shows seek-observed entries distinguishably from swept ones (ask the Designer if not obvious).

## Questions for the Builder to answer first (read the code)

- How does `StartScanAsync` decide a station is present (threshold, dwell)? Is that the same criterion the
  sweep uses? If they differ, a seek-observed entry and a swept one are not comparable. Say so and pick one.
- Does a map-driven jump need the same signal check on arrival (station gone since the sweep)? If the
  tuned channel is dead, continue to the next mapped one or fall back to live seek. Decide which.
- AM/SW/AIR/WB/VHF: which bands are mappable (`BandSweepPlans.UnavailableReason`)? Unmappable bands keep
  live seek only.
- Interaction with `AUD-95` (frequency-only tune into another band) and `AUD-91` (sweep follows the band).

## Done when

- Unit tests: map-hit jump each direction, wrap, stale/absent map fallback, seek upsert that does not
  touch sweep time/age, full sweep replaces seek-observed entries.
- Box UAT: sweep FM, then Scan Up lands on the next mapped station with no audible seek; delete or age the
  map, then Scan Up still works via live seek and the stop appears on the BAND view as seek-observed.

## Builder's answers (read from the code, 2026-10-04)

**1. How the seek decides a station is there, and whether the sweep uses the same rule. It does not, and the two
are not comparable.** `RadioReceiver.ScanInternal` steps by the source's step, dwells 150 ms, and stops when
`_lastSignalStrength >= ScanStopThreshold / 100`. `_lastSignalStrength` is the RMS magnitude of about 256 raw IQ
samples of the **whole 2.4 MS/s capture** (`ProcessSamples`), taken behind the tuner's AGC. It is wideband (±1.2 MHz
around the tuned frequency), linear, and roughly 0 to 1.4, so a strong station lifts it for every frequency nearby.
The sweep measures **in-channel** power (±80 kHz on FM, DC excluded) in relative dB at a fixed 28 dB gain. A
channel counts as a station when it is a local maximum at least 6 dB above the map's median, which is the BAND
view's tap rule. **Picked: the sweep's rule decides the map's stations.** It now lives in one place,
`Radio.Core.Models.BandMapStations.Peaks`, and the Web tap calls it. The seek's reading is kept only as a seek-observed
entry's own `SeekStrength`, never mixed into the dB channels or compared with them.

**2. No signal check on arrival at a mapped station; a dead one costs one pause, and the scan moves on.** The only
live measurement is the seek's wideband RMS (above). Gating map stations on it would bring back exactly the criterion
the map exists to replace. On the box it would also skip almost everything, because `ScanStopThreshold` there is 100
(threshold 1.00), which only clipping signals reach (§ Box UAT). The scan is continuous, like the live seek: 2 s on
each station, then the next one. So a station gone since the sweep is heard as 2 s of noise, and then the next mapped
station plays. It never falls back to live seek in the middle of a scan. Staleness is bounded by
`BandMap:ScanMapMaxAgeMinutes` instead.

**3. Mappable bands: FM, WB, AIR, VHF** (`BandSweepPlans.UnavailableReason` is null for these). **AM and SW are
not mappable**: they are below the R820T's 24 MHz floor. A custom band (a frequency tune outside every preset)
has no band code. All three keep live seek only, and their seek stops are not recorded.

**4. `AUD-95` and `AUD-91`.**
- `AUD-91`: the map Scan reads is the receiver's current band. A frequency outside the range the map swept falls
  back to live seek (`outside-map`). This covers the VHF window that follows the radio.
- `AUD-95`: a frequency-only tune into another band does move the receiver's band (only the step is stale), so
  the map-driven scan reads the right band. It hops by the map, not by the step, so the stale step does not affect
  it. A live seek after such a tune still steps by the stale step, so its seek-observed entries can sit off the
  channel grid. That is harmless: they are drawn where they are, and a peak within one channel spacing wins over
  them in the scan list.

**Freshness threshold: 1440 minutes (a day), configurable as `BandMap:ScanMapMaxAgeMinutes`; 0 means always seek
live.** The timer re-sweeps the radio's band hourly when the dongle is idle or the console is asleep, but not while
the radio plays with the console awake. Broadcast transmitters do not move within a day. A stale map costs a new
station being skipped and a dead one costing one pause; beyond a day, live seek is the safer default.

## What shipped

- **Scan reads the map.** `SDRRadioAudioSource.StartScanAsync` asks `IScanStationMap.GetScanStations`
  (`BandMapService`). The list is the map's peaks plus its seek-observed stations at least one channel spacing from
  every peak; a peak wins over a seek-observed station near it. `RadioReceiver.ScanStations` hops between them in
  the scan's direction, wraps at the band edge, pauses 2 s on each, and stops when the next hop would reach or pass
  where it started, or when the band changes. Falls back to the live seek when there is no map (`no-map`), the map is
  stale (`map-stale`), it does not cover the frequency (`outside-map`), it lists no other station (`no-other-station`),
  the band is not mappable, or the map cannot be read. The reason is logged at Information in
  `Radio.Infrastructure.Audio`, which LOG-2 holds at Warning by default.
- **Scan writes the map.** Each stop of a live seek (`RadioReceiver.SeekStationFound`) is upserted by frequency
  into `bandmap/<band>-seek.json`, beside the map and never in it. Swept channels, `scannedAtUtc` and age are never
  touched, and a band with only seek-observed stations still has no map (`GET` reports null scan time and age, and
  no channels). The timer's due logic reads only the maps, so a band with only seek stops is still swept on time.
  A completed sweep drops the seek-observed stations in its range that were observed no later than it finished.
- **BAND view.** `GET /api/radio/bandmap` has `seekStations` (`frequencyHz`, `seekStrength`, `observedAtUtc`).
  They are drawn as a hollow ring at the top of the plot over a dashed line to the axis, with no height, keyed
  "found by scan". On a never-swept band the view still says "No stations discovered yet", with *"The rings mark
  where Scan stopped. Tap Discover to sweep the whole band."* The view re-reads the map on the tick a scan ends.

## Box UAT (agent, 2026-10-04, `d0f7a9e` on both services)

1. **Sweep FM**: `POST /api/radio/bandmap/scan?band=FM` → completed, 101 channels, 20 peaks (90.5 … 105.5).
2. **Map-driven scan.** All three were logged as *"hops between the 20 stations of the FM band map"*, with no frequency
   between stations ever reported by `/api/radio/state`:
   - Scan Up from 92.3: 92.9 → 93.7 → 94.7 → 95.1, about 2 s apart.
   - Scan Down from 95.1: 94.7 → 93.7 → 92.9.
   - Scan Up from 105.5 (the top peak) wrapped: 90.5 → 91.5.
3. **Fallback.** WB's map was 3.1 days old. Scan Up on WB logged *"seeks live on band WB (map-stale)"* and stepped
   through every 25 kHz channel. The WB map's `scannedAtUtc` (2026-10-01 08:53:48) was unchanged afterwards.
   ⚠ **The seek-observed upsert was NOT exercised on the box.** The live seek made no stop: the box's
   `ScanStopThreshold` is **100** (threshold 1.00, `Starting continuous scan … threshold=1.00`), which only a clipping
   signal reaches, and none on WB did. Making FM map-less to force stops needed a remote file move and an API restart,
   which the session's permission policy refused. The upsert, its persistence, and the map, time and age it must not
   touch are covered by unit tests only (`BandMapServiceScanTests`, `SDRRadioAudioSourceScanTests`,
   `BandMapControllerBandTests`).

**Finding (not fixed, owner/Planner call):** the box's config store holds both `radio:scanStopThreshold = 50` and
`radio:ScanStopThreshold = 100`, the same case-variant pair `UI-28` met. With 100 in effect, the live seek on the box
stops only on clipping signals. That means it rarely records anything, and on bands without a fresh map, Scan rarely
pauses at all.
