# `AUD-100` — Scan Up/Down and the band map share one station list

[← Builder Queue index](../BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

🟡 **Owner request, 2026-10-04.** NEW, not started.

> *"There are a couple of ways to scan for stations … Scan Up/Scan Down buttons on the radio control
> console, and … the 'Discover' button in the radio bandwidth visualization panel … This feels a bit like
> duplication."* Owner chose options 1 + 2 of the coordinator's proposal: keep both controls; connect their data.

## The two flows today (read from the controllers only — internals not yet read)

| | Scan Up/Down | Discover |
|---|---|---|
| Endpoint | `POST /api/radio/scan/start`, `/scan/stop` (`RadioController.cs:263`, `:298`) | `POST /api/bandmap/scan` (`BandMapController.cs:69`) |
| Does | Live seek from the current frequency to the next station | Whole-band sweep, output silenced (~21 s FM) |
| Keeps | Nothing | `BandMap` per band: channels, signal, scan time, age (`BandMapService`) |

Neither reads the other's results.

## What to build

1. **Scan reads the map.** When the current band has a map younger than a freshness threshold, Scan
   Up/Down tunes straight to the next mapped channel above/below the current frequency (wrapping at the band
   edge as the live seek does today). No map, a stale map, or no mapped channel in that direction → fall back
   to today's live seek. Pick the threshold and justify it; make it configuration.
2. **Scan writes the map.** Each station a live seek stops on is upserted into that band's map with its
   signal and a timestamp, marked as **seek-observed**, not swept.
   ⚠ **A seek proves presence, never absence.** It skips frequencies without measuring them, so a
   seek-observed entry must not remove or override swept channels, must not reset the map's sweep time or
   age, and must not make a never-swept band look swept. A later full sweep is the authority and replaces
   seek-observed entries.
3. The BAND view shows seek-observed entries distinguishably from swept ones (ask the Designer if not obvious).

## Questions for the Builder to answer first (read the code)

- How does `StartScanAsync` decide a station is present (threshold, dwell)? Is that the same criterion the
  sweep uses? If they differ, a seek-observed entry and a swept one are not comparable. Say so and pick one.
- Does a map-driven jump need the same signal check on arrival (station gone since the sweep)? If the
  tuned channel is dead, continue to the next mapped one or fall back to live seek. Decide which.
- AM/SW/AIR/WB/VHF: which bands are mappable (`BandSweepPlans.UnavailableReason`)? Unmappable bands keep
  live seek only.
- Interaction with `AUD-95` (frequency-only tune into another band) and `AUD-91` (sweep follows the band).

## Done when

- Unit tests: map-hit jump each direction, wrap, stale/absent map fallback, seek upsert that does not
  touch sweep time/age, full sweep replaces seek-observed entries.
- Box UAT: sweep FM, then Scan Up lands on the next mapped station with no audible seek; delete or age the
  map, then Scan Up still works via live seek and the stop appears on the BAND view as seek-observed.
