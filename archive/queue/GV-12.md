# `GV-12` — the phone surface never retries, so a transient outage leaves it dead until a human taps Retry

[← Builder Queue index](../BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

🟠 **P1.** Filed 2026-09-08. **Found by the RotaryPhone session while debugging their own outage** —
they were reading our logs alongside theirs and noticed what we did not.

## The evidence, confirmed from both ends

RotaryPhone's GV bridge was dead 14:08–15:31 EDT (~83 minutes). Service was restored at **15:31:17**.

**After restoration, `radio-web` made ZERO further GV calls.** No `GvBridgeApiService` activity in our
logs; no inbound SMS/voicemail requests in theirs. The UI sat on *"Couldn't load…"* against a **fully
healthy backend** until the owner tapped **Retry**, which worked immediately.

**So an 83-minute upstream outage leaves our phone surface permanently dead until a human intervenes,
even after the cause has cleared.**

⭐ It is the mirror image of the honest-status work: **we made failure visible, and then built a
surface that cannot recover from having seen it.**

## What to build

An auto-retry with backoff, or a refetch when the reconnect banner clears. Prefer the latter if the
banner is being touched anyway — one trigger beats two clocks.

## ⛔ The status contract we were given this morning is WRONG — do not use it

RotaryPhone's morning reply (`inbound/2026-09-08-rotaryphone-reply.md:98`) told us to bind to
`degraded` or `authBlackout` and **never** to `available`. **They retracted it the same day**, and the
outage proved it. Live capture during the total outage, while we were getting 502s:

```json
{"available":false,"degraded":false,"authBlackout":false,
 "cookiesValid":false,"lastApiSuccessAt":null}
```

**`degraded` and `authBlackout` both false through a total outage.** A banner bound to either would
have stayed silent for 83 minutes. There are two failure states and that advice covered one: when the
adapter goes **inactive**, the honest fields reset to false because they are **per-activation**.

**Bind to the shape, not to a boolean:**

```
unhealthy = !cookiesValid || !available || degraded || authBlackout
            || lastApiSuccessAt is null or older than ~2 min
```

`cookiesValid:false` held in both states; `lastApiSuccessAt` is the strongest single signal. And
**`authBlackout` can be true for under a second** (920 ms measured, zero true-samples across 411
polls), so **latch it as an event with a minimum display window** — a 10-second poll will never see it.

## ⚠ Read `UI-10` before estimating this

`radio-web`'s own Blazor circuit was timing out every ~30 s throughout, with a
`JSDisconnectedException` at 15:27:06. **A dead circuit cannot refetch even if the code wants to**, so
part of what looks like a missing-retry defect may be a broken transport. Establish which before
building — a retry bolted onto a dying circuit will look like it does not work, and the wrong row will
take the blame.

## Verification

Unit-testable with a fake API service: fail N times, then succeed, and assert the panel refetches
**without user input**. ⚠ **Must fail first** — today it does not refetch at all, so confirm the test
is RED against `main` before trusting it.

⚠ Live confirmation needs the bridge to actually go down and come back. **Do not simulate that on the
box.** The next real outage is the honest test, and this row should say so rather than implying a green
unit test proves recovery.

## ⭐ NARROWED 2026-09-08 by RotaryPhone — the panels DO fetch on mount

**They predicted this row would reproduce on our 16:07 deploy, it did not, and they said so unprompted.**

Measured after the restart: `radio-web ExecMainStartTimestamp 16:07:29 EDT`, and **6 inbound
SMS/voicemail requests** to them since. **The panels fetched and were served.**

⚠ **So the row as filed pointed at the wrong layer.** "The panels never fetch" invites someone to
investigate the mount path — **which is the one part working correctly.**

**The corrected defect: the panels do not RE-FETCH after a failure that occurs while the circuit stays
up.** They fetch on mount and never again. This morning differed in exactly the way that matters —
`radio-web` stayed up for the whole 83 minutes, so components had already mounted, already rendered
their error state, and nothing ever caused them to try again.

### What follows for the build

- **The fix is a retry loop or a refetch trigger, not a mount-path change.** Do not touch mounting.
- ⭐ **A `radio-web` restart is a real mitigation** — ugly, but any deploy incidentally clears a stuck
  panel. Worth knowing while the proper fix is queued.
- ⭐ **`UI-10` being upstream now looks STRONGER, not weaker.** A circuit that drops and re-establishes
  cleanly produces a re-mount and therefore a fetch; **a circuit that hangs half-dead produces
  neither.** Establishing which of those our 30-second timeouts actually cause is the first question.
- **A trigger that costs nothing: refetch when the reconnect banner clears.** That is precisely the
  transition at which a stuck panel becomes wrong, and we already compute it.

## ✅ SHIPPED 2026-09-08 — what the build changed, and the four row premises it falsified

**Merged as [#631](https://github.com/mmackelprang/RTest/pull/631).** ⚠ **Merged, not shipped** — the
appliance was 13 PRs behind at merge time and was deliberately not deployed for this row.

**The fix.** `GvBridgeHealth.IsHealthy` (a pure predicate over the fuller status shape) feeds
`GvBridgeStatusService.IsHealthy`; `PhonePage.OnGvStatusChanged` refetches on its unhealthy→healthy
**edge**, and an error-gated backstop rides the existing 5 s timer for the case the edge cannot see.
No new clock. Full reasoning in [ADR-032](../../design/DECISION-LOG.md).

### Four things this row asserted that turned out to be wrong

1. ⛔ **"Bind to the shape: `!cookiesValid || !available || degraded || authBlackout ||
   lastApiSuccessAt null-or-stale`" is right for a BANNER and would have made this fix a silent
   no-op.** A recovery **edge** must be able to reach healthy; any permanently-true unhealthy term
   pins the state and the edge never fires. `cookiesValid` was a non-nullable `bool` defaulting to
   `false`, so it was live-pinned against the DTO as it stood. The shipped predicate is
   deliberately asymmetric — **present-and-bad** makes it unhealthy, **absent** contributes nothing.
2. ⛔ **`lastApiSuccessAt`'s null-is-unhealthy rule is inverted for this use.** It was `null`
   throughout the outage, so under the row's own rule it would have contributed nothing to detection
   anyway — while in steady state it is the single term most able to pin the predicate. It is kept,
   but null is no signal, and the 2-minute gate is now backed by a **measurement**, not a guess:
   `lastApiSuccessAt` advances on a **60 s** cadence (measured on `radio`, 2026-09-09T01:58Z–02:02Z),
   a 2× margin, pinned by `MeasuredSixtySecondCadence_StaysHealthy`.
3. ⛔ **"Latch `authBlackout` with a minimum display window"** is correct for a banner and actively
   wrong here — a latch holds the state unhealthy past the moment health returns, delaying or
   suppressing the very edge being detected. Included as a term, not latched.
4. ⛔ **"`UI-10` being upstream now looks STRONGER"** — it is not upstream at all, and the reason
   given (*"a circuit that drops and re-establishes cleanly produces a re-mount and therefore a
   fetch"*) is **false for Blazor Server**. A reconnected circuit resumes the same component
   instances with the same fields. Neither branch produces a fetch. That is what makes the refetch
   trigger the *only* recovery mechanism, not a belt-and-braces addition.

### And one the PLAN got wrong, found during the build

⛔ The plan said whether RotaryPhone serves `degraded` / `authBlackout` / `lastApiSuccessAt`
**"could not be verified from this tree"**, and that absent fields would degrade the predicate to
`!Available`. **Both halves false** — `inbound/2026-09-08-rotaryphone-reply.md:87-91` carries all
three, and the box confirmed it live. Because they *are* served, the live predicate includes the
staleness gate, which is how premise 2 above became load-bearing.

### Verification

**RED first, as its own commit** — both regression tests failed against the unfixed page
(*"expected a refetch after recovery; thread-list calls = 1"*).

⭐ **Then proven in a real browser with a real Blazor circuit**, against a local stub serving this
row's verbatim outage body. **RotaryPhone was never touched.** With the backstop provably disarmed
(a populated list keeps `_threadsError` false, so only the edge can fire): thread calls frozen for
46 s with the bridge down, then exactly one refetch per list **~6.2 s after restore, with nobody
touching the browser**, banner cleared, recovered row rendered, zero console errors.

⛔ **Still not proven, and not claimed:** that RotaryPhone's real recovery path reports the edge the
way the stub does. **The next real outage is that test.** Do not manufacture one.
