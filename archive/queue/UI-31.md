## What changed

- **The tab reads RADIO** (`Radio`, upper-cased by the picker's CSS like its siblings). Only user-facing text: the label, `aria-label` "Radio signal map mode", and the canvas
  label "FM signal map. Touch a signal to tune." (now with `role="img"`, so the label is not ignored) **The saved value is unchanged** — `VisualizationMode.Band`
  keeps its name, so `ui.visualizer/defaultMode` still stores `Band` and the owner's saved `Band` still opens the
  view (renaming it would orphan the stored value — the `AUD-1` lesson). `/api/radio/bandmap…` is unchanged.
- **No tooltip on the greyed tab** (the `title` `UI-29` added). The screen-reader description stays.
- **The 64 px bar over the top of the plot is gone.** A **91 px strip under the canvas** (its top edge level with the preset bar's) holds, left to right:
  the band's name (32 px) and range (the control panel pill's own `Range`, from `GET /api/RadioBands`; VHF shows
  `WINDOW` and the plotted window); the status (`DISCOVERED 3 MIN AGO`, `DISCOVERING… 12 S`,
  `DISCOVERING AIR… 12 S`, `OUT OF RANGE`) with the `UI-22` colour key on the right; the help text — *"Touch a
  signal to snap to it. ▲ marks a preset."* / *"Fine-tune with the tuner's ‹ › buttons or the knob."* (the glyphs
  are hidden from screen readers, with words read in their place) — which a tune or discover message replaces for
  its 4 s; and **Discover** (was Scan), 112 × 60 px, level with the preset bar's arrows.
- **The axis** is drawn in the canvas's bottom 32 px (line + 6 px ticks) with 13 px `--text-medium` labels laid
  over it (was a 10 px `--text-low` strip). Labels carry no unit — the range line does — which also ends the
  clipped `162.55 MHz` on WB and VHF. No axis on AM/SW.
- **Presets are carets** (10 × 8 px, `--text-high`) pointing up at the axis; the dashed full-height lines and the
  two lanes of preset labels are gone (the Designer's recommendation; owner check below). The tuned station keeps
  its amber line and gets a 16 × 12 px amber caret on the axis.
- **Plot height is about the same** (≈ 425 px before and after): the strip is paid for by the deleted top bar and label lanes.
- **A tap anywhere on the canvas tunes** — nothing overlays it any more, so the top-64 px ignore zone is gone.
- **Accessibility:** tune confirmations go to one persistent polite `role="status"` region; errors render as
  `role="alert"`. The status line (its countdown re-renders every second) is not live.
- **Found while rendering, fixed here:** the canvas buffer was sized once at `visualizer.init` and never again,
  so a mode whose layout differs from the start-up mode was drawn stretched. With the strip that was a 14 %
  squash (539 px buffer shown at 466 px) and an axis off its labels. `ensureCanvasSize` now follows the element's
  laid-out size in every mode, and while the map is shown a `ResizeObserver` repaints it when the size changes
  (the map is drawn on data, not per frame).

## Not changed (deliberately)

- The API's own sentences still say "scan": AM/SW's reason *"…and cannot be scanned."* (`BandSweepPlans`, in the
  `RTLSDRCore` package) and the 409 texts *"This band cannot be scanned"* / *"The AIR band is being scanned; try
  again when it finishes"* (`BandMapController`). They are shown verbatim; the API was out of this row's scope.
  The 409s are reachable only in a race, since Discover is disabled during any sweep and on AM/SW.

## Tests

`VisualizerPanelBandTests` — existing expectations moved to the new copy and markup, plus 14 for `UI-31`:
band name and pill range; the band list read once and kept; fallback to the plotted range when the band list fails, replaced once a retry succeeds; VHF window; help text;
a tune message replaces the help in the persistent polite region and the help returns on expiry; an error is
an alert outside it; out of range (status, no help, no axis, Discover in place and disabled, draw model
`mappable: false`); presets in the draw model carry no label; Discover is the strip's only button and nothing
over the canvas is a button; **no "scan" in the view's text or accessible names** across five states; the saved
`Band` preference opens RADIO and a pick of RADIO saves `"Band"` exactly. `VisualizerPanelTests` — the tab
label, its `aria-label`, no `title`. `BandAxisTests` — no label carries a unit. `BandMapTextTests` —
`FormatDiscovering`, `FormatRange`. Mutation results are in the PR body. The band list is read with `GetBandsForPollAsync`, which logs a failure at Debug: it is retried on the 30 s refresh until it succeeds, and `radio-web`'s Information and above reach journald (review finding). ⚠ The JS (caret drawing, the canvas
sizing, the resize repaint) has no unit test; it was checked by rendering at 1920 × 720 and measuring the
canvas buffer against its layout.
