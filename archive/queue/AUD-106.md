# `AUD-106` — FM stereo/RDS-loss watchdog: catch, instrument and self-recover a receiver that streams IQ but decodes no pilot and no RDS

[← Builder Queue index](../../docs/BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

🟡 **P2. Filed 2026-10-12 by the `AUD-99` Builder** on the owner's ruling on [#800](https://github.com/mmackelprang/RTest/pull/800):
*"Merge + watchdog row."* The fault is accepted as intermittent and possibly pre-existing. This row exists so the
next occurrence is caught, explained and recovered without a person restarting the service.

## The one observed occurrence (MEASURED, box `radio`, build `5cc20e5`, 2026-10-12 UTC)

- **Before:** about 7 minutes on AM (HF direct sampling, Q branch). Eight `POST /api/radio/frequency` AM tunes
  45 s apart (1090, 1180, 750, 1400, 1190, 1650, 710, 1030 kHz), with `Radio.Infrastructure.Audio` raised to
  Debug. Earlier in the same process: AM, SW 9.6 MHz, back to FM; an FM live sweep (after which `gain` read 28,
  see `AUD-90`); a 409 FM-scan refusal while on AM; and 5 AM↔FM switches in 2 s. FM was healthy after all of
  those.
- **00:15:19.908 UTC:** `POST /api/radio/frequency 97745000` switched to FM. The file sink logged
  `Switching to FM Broadcast (pausing stream)`,
  `HF direct sampling OFF (mode 0 read back); tuner active`,
  `Signal processing: … Stereo decode …`,
  and `Starting RTL-SDR sample streaming` at 00:15:20.359.
  **No `RDS: Block sync acquired` followed.** On a good return it follows within about 0.4 s.
- **After:** for more than 2 minutes, `/api/radio/state` read `isStereo:false`, `rdsPi:null`,
  `rdsStationNameStable:null`, with signalStrength 100 (clip). FM→FM retunes at 00:16:54, 00:17:42 and 00:17:58 to
  97.745, 94.7 (signalStrength 37) and 92.3 MHz (100) did not recover it. signalStrength changed per station, so
  IQ was flowing and `ProcessSamples` was running. `isHfMode` read false. No Warning or Error in journald for
  `radio-api` in that window.
- **Recovery:** a service restart (here, the deploy of `0028577` at about 00:20 UTC). After it: stereo, PI 34174,
  `WSMW` at once.

## Hypotheses (none tested)

1. **R820T or RTL2832 register state** left by `AUD-99`'s HF-off path on unlucky timing (librtlsdr's HF-off runs
   the tuner's `init`, then `set_if_freq`, spectrum inversion and `set_center_freq`). If the IF or the inversion
   were wrong, FM would demodulate off-centre: signal present, no 19 kHz pilot, no RDS. That fits the symptoms.
2. **Receiver DSP state** that survives `SetBand`: a stereo decoder or RDS decoder object not rebuilt or reset.
3. **An older intermittent fault** that band changes happen to expose.

## Scope

1. **Instrument first.** Log once, at Information to the file sink:
   - every FM (re)start's outcome: pilot detected (or not) and RDS sync (or not) within N seconds;
   - on a miss, a one-shot diagnostic snapshot: the device's direct-sampling mode (`rtlsdr_get_direct_sampling`),
     tuner type, centre frequency, sample rate, gain mode/gain, the pilot-detector level, the stereo/RDS
     decoder states, and the IQ DC offset and I/Q power ratio over one block. The snapshot is meant to tell
     hypothesis 1 apart from 2.
2. **Watchdog.** On FM, when signalStrength is above a threshold but there is no pilot and no RDS for T seconds
   on a station that had either before (or on any station, after a band change), escalate:
   - (a) re-run the band setup (HF off, gain, sample rate, retune) without closing the device;
   - (b) close and reopen the device (the in-process equivalent of the restart that recovered it).

   Log each step once at Warning; it is a real fault. Rate-limit it, and never loop. A mono station without RDS
   must not trigger it: key on *had pilot or RDS before on this frequency*, or on a band change, and decide this
   in the plan.
3. **Tests:** a fake device that streams IQ with no pilot after a band change triggers the watchdog once,
   recovers, and does not re-trigger; a genuinely mono station does not trigger it. Counted, not timed.

## Shipped 2026-10-12 — instrument, watchdog, recovery

### Decisions

These are the plan decisions the dossier left open.

- **What is judged.** Only acquisitions are judged: every FM start (start-up, band change, frequency tune,
  modulation change, a watchdog step) and every retune inside FM. Steady listening is never judged, so a fade
  or a mono programme cannot interrupt a station. A window is 3 s. It closes early once both the pilot and RDS
  are seen. Squelched, muted and sweep blocks are never observed.
- **Trigger key.** All three must hold:
  1. Neither the pilot nor RDS appears in the window.
  2. The mean wideband signal is at least 0.1.
  3. The frequency was **locked earlier in this process**.

  "Locked" means RDS sync, or 8 consecutive blocks with the pilot in phase with the PLL (in-phase at least 0.02
  and at least 0.5 × the band energy). The stereo detector alone is not enough, because it reads noise as a
  pilot (`AUD-109`). A genuinely mono station without RDS is therefore never recovered, only reported. The cost
  is that a fault on a frequency's first acquisition after a restart is reported, not recovered.
- **Escalation per episode.** Each step logs once at Warning:
  1. Re-run the band setup: HF on and off (librtlsdr's HF-off path re-initialises the tuner), DSP chain,
     sample rate, tune, gain. If HF is still on after the cycle, close and reopen at once.
  2. Close and reopen the device.
  3. Give up.

  An episode ends on a healthy acquisition, or on an FM start the watchdog did not cause. At most 3 episodes
  run per hour. A queued step is skipped if the receiver was retuned or rebuilt, left FM, or is sweeping. The
  steps run off the DSP thread, chained one after another.
- **Instrumentation.** One Information line goes to the file sink per FM start, with:
  - the pilot and RDS times;
  - the pilot level, in-phase component and PLL;
  - the mean signal.

  On a miss, a snapshot adds:
  - the IQ DC offset and I/Q power ratio;
  - the composite mean (× 75 kHz gives the carrier offset, the evidence for hypothesis 1);
  - librtlsdr's *recorded* mode, centre, rate and gain. These are not register reads, and the snapshot says
    so.

  For hypothesis 1, also check journald for `[R82XX] PLL not locked!`.
- **`AUD-90`.** The snapshot logs both the tuner gain and the receiver's auto/manual gain, and recovery
  re-applies the receiver's gain mode.

### Reviews

There were three adversarial passes on the session model.

- **Pass 1** found two HIGH problems. A block queued from the old tune turned a start into a retune. The
  snapshot claimed to read the live device. It also found four MEDIUM problems: step 1's HF re-run was a no-op
  from the cache, a step could hit a station the user had moved to, a failed reopen half tore down the
  receiver, and "known" rested on an energy detector.
- **Pass 2** found one HIGH problem: the in-phase test alone read noise as locked at 7–37 % of block ends, in
  the reviewer's simulation. It also found two MEDIUM problems: the HF cycle could strand HF on, and a failed
  resume did not tear down.
- All of these are fixed, with tests. The remaining LOW: `SetModulation` publishes a chain without pausing
  streaming, so its start can still read as a retune. It has no production caller.
