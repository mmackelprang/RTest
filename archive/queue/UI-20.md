## What the owner asked (2026-10-01)

> *"I'd like to consider moving the preset column to a bar across the bottom of the radio with
> touchable arrow pushbuttons to scroll left and right. This would widen the radio control area,
> hopefully providing enough horizontal space for all band buttons on one row, allowing space for a
> preset band above the current two-row band selection area. … It also seems like the scan area could
> be color coded based on signal strength. Consider that as part of a UX enhancement also."*

The colour half is [`UI-22`](UI-22.md). The spec recommends the bar at the **bottom** rather than above
the bands (§3); either way the vertical room comes from the band row collapsing to one row.

## Owner decisions that bind this row (spec, top section)

- **Q1 — four wider cards** (≈129 × 60 px), not five narrower.
- **Q2 — yes, the empty-slot card is a tappable `＋ SAVE`.** It opens the same save dialog as the
  long-press. Long-press on the active band pill still saves. ⚠ This **reverses** the radio-controller
  handoff's save-by-long-press-only choice (`design_handoff_radio_controller/IMPLEMENTATION.md:254-256`)
  **by owner decision**. When the current station is already saved the placeholder does not render
  (spec §4.6).
- **Q3 — no `⋮` kebab on bar cards; long-press a card (600 ms) for Rename / Delete.** Same menu and
  undo toast as today. The `RadioPage.razor` `Card` variant keeps its kebab (out of scope).
- **Q4 is NOT decided** — AM and SW behave exactly as today, just in one row. Their dim/block treatment
  waits on [`AUD-94`](AUD-94.md) and is not part of this PR.

## Scope (spec §4)

- **Remove the 210 px rail** (`RadioControlPanel.razor:840` `.rcp-presets { width: 210px }`). `.rcp-root`
  becomes a column; the tuner rows (RDS, frequency well, RSSI, AGC) widen from `max-width: 420` to the
  full ≈634 px content column (§4.1).
- **Band buttons on one row:** six equal pills, `flex: 1`, ≈102 × 56 px each, same two-line content and
  amber active state (§2.2, §4.3). Long-press of the active pill still saves.
- **Preset bar (§4.5):** full `.rcp-root` width, pinned to the bottom, rail colours kept.
  - Caption row: `PRESETS · N saved`, right side `HOLD [FM] TO SAVE` or, when paged, `1–4 of 8`.
  - **◀ / ▶ arrows 58 × 60**, console push-button styling (the `.rcp-tune-btn` bevel). **Tap = one
    page** (viewport width, snapped to a card edge); **hold = auto-repeat** one page every 400 ms after
    600 ms. Disabled at the ends at 0.4 opacity with `aria-disabled`, never removed (no reflow).
  - **Swipe / drag** with `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`, `touch-action: pan-x`; > 10 px of movement
    cancels the tap and the long-press. No scrollbar — a 2 px position track in the bottom padding.
  - **Four cards ≈129 × 60:** `PresetCard` variant `Bar`. Name up to **two lines** then ellipsis (full
    name in `title` and the aria label); meta line pinned to the bottom, `06 · FM 92.30` (ordinal in
    `--text-medium`, not `--text-low`, for AA). Band-group separator: 14 px gap with a hairline.
  - **Active preset** uses today's `.is-active` treatment and today's test (band equal, |Δf| < 1 Hz —
    the knob's `IsCurrent`). **Auto-scroll** the least distance that shows it when it changes (200 ms
    smooth; instant under `prefers-reduced-motion`) — **except within 10 s of a manual swipe or arrow
    tap**. When it is off-screen, the arrow on that side carries a **6 px amber dot** with
    `aria-describedby` text.
  - On band change, scroll to the active preset, else the first card of the new band's group, else that
    band's placeholder. **Do not reorder** — band then slot, which is the knob's order
    (`PresetSelectorService.cs:262-266`, "matches the on-screen rail exactly").
- **States (§7):** loading skeleton gains a one-row band strip and four card ghosts; a failed preset read
  shows an inert `Couldn't load presets` / `tap to retry` card (today it shows a false `NO PRESETS`);
  no-presets card; 1–4 presets with both arrows disabled; 20+ (max 50) paged.
- **Accessibility (§8):** `role="region"` `aria-label="Presets"`, `role="list"`, per-card aria labels
  with `aria-current` on the active one, arrow `aria-controls`, position text not a live region.

## ⚠ Pre-existing bug this row fixes (spec §2.3, §4.4)

During a tuner scan the `SCANNING UP` / `SIGNAL −12 dBu` row (`.rcp-scanning` / `.rcp-scan-signal`,
`RadioControlPanel.razor:156-171`) inserts ~36 px against **16 px** of slack today, so the AGC row is
pushed ~20 px past the bottom and clipped (`.rcp-tuner` is `overflow: hidden`). **Fix in this PR:** move
that text into the RSSI meter's header row, between "RSSI" and the dBu readout, keeping the amber
blink / green pulse at the header's 9 px mono. The scan buttons already collapse to a red STOP, so the
state stays visible. With the bar added there is no slack left at all, so this is not optional.
