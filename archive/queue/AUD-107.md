# `AUD-107` — NFM (Weather, VHF): add the IQ channel filter that `AUD-101` added for AM

[← Builder Queue index](../../docs/BUILDER_QUEUE.md)

🟢 **P3. Filed 2026-10-12 by the `AUD-101` fixer**, as the follow-up `AUD-101` § *Out of scope* asked for. Read
from code on `feat/aud-101-am-dsp-chain`; **nothing in this row has been measured on the box.** No listener has
reported a fault on Weather or VHF.

## Measured facts (code)

- **No IQ channel filter on NFM.** `CalculateRates` (`src/RTLSDRCore/RadioReceiver.cs`, `CalculateRates`) picks
  the smallest multiple of 48 kHz at or above the channel bandwidth and then clamps the SDR rate to at least
  225 kHz, raising the demod rate to match. For Weather and VHF (`BandPresets.cs`: NFM, `DefaultBandwidthHz =
  12_500`) that gives SDR rate = demod rate = **240 kHz**, so the IQ decimator is not built and the FM
  demodulator sees the **whole ±120 kHz capture**, not the 12.5 kHz channel. Adjacent channels and wideband noise
  reach the discriminator.
- **Same defect `AUD-101` fixed for AM.** `AUD-101` left NFM alone on purpose (out of scope: it changes bands that
  work today) and noted this as a follow-up.
- **Tuned on the carrier.** NFM is tuned zero-IF (the device centre is the station); unlike AM in HF mode, the
  tuner is active here, so the DC spike is less of a concern, but it still lands on the carrier.

## Pattern to follow

`AUD-101`'s `AmChannelizer` (`src/RTLSDRCore/DSP/AmChannelizer.cs`): one pass that mixes by a table NCO, filters
with a Hamming-sinc FIR and decimates, evaluating the FIR only for the kept samples. It is wired as a chain of its
own (`AmReceiveChain`, built from `SetupSignalProcessing`) rather than through `CalculateRates`, which also sets
the WFM path. For NFM the same channelizer could filter to about ±6.25 kHz and decimate 240 → 48 kHz before the
FM demodulator; whether to add an offset too (as AM does) is for the plan.

⚠ **Keep the level scale.** `AUD-101` moved AIR back to the wideband linear `SignalStrength` after the box showed
the tuner's AGC holds the in-channel noise at −18 to −26 dBFS. Weather and VHF are tuner-mode bands too: do not
change their squelch or seek scale without box numbers.

## Scope

1. A channel filter and decimation for NFM (Weather, VHF), as above.
2. Tests through the whole `RadioReceiver` with a fake device (the `AUD-101` harness in
   `tests/RTLSDRCore.Tests/AmChain/`): an NFM tone is recovered; an adjacent NFM station 12.5 or 25 kHz away is
   rejected by a stated number of dB; the WFM golden test (`Wfm_Output_IsUnchanged`) still passes.
3. Box check: NOAA Weather (162.4–162.55 MHz) is audible and no worse than before; CPU on NFM vs FM.

## Out of scope

The signal-strength scale on NFM bands (see the warning above); WFM.

## Depends on

`AUD-101` (the channelizer and the test harness).

## Shipped 2026-10-12 — [#805](https://github.com/mmackelprang/RTest/pull/805)

**What shipped:**
- `NfmReceiveChain` uses `IqChannelizer`, which was `AmChannelizer`, with no offset.
- The filter: −6 dB at 0.64 × the channel bandwidth (8 kHz) and a transition of 0.32 × (4 kHz). That makes it flat to
  6 kHz with the stop band from 10 kHz, at 199 taps.
- It decimates 240 → 48 kHz, and the discriminator runs at 48 kHz.

**Unchanged on purpose:**
- Zero-IF tuning.
- The wideband signal strength, so squelch and seek are unaffected.
- The audio scale.

**Synthetic tests** through the whole receiver, before (chain disabled) → after:

|---|---|---|

| 12.5 kHz neighbour residual | +16.8 dB | −16.8 dB |
| Wideband noise residual (CNR about 15 dB in the channel) | +1.9 dB | −11.5 dB |

`Wfm_Output_IsUnchanged` still passes.
